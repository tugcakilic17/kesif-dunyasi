# Dördüncü harita — ormandan adalar ve kumsala

Yerleşik `image_gen` kullanıldı. Uygulama yalnızca dördüncü haritayı açar; yuvarlak oyun noktaları kaldırılmıştır. Harita, değiştirilmeyen `forest-valley-original.png` kaynağıyla başlar. Aşağıdaki istemler ilk üretim aşamasının kaydıdır; güncel bahçe ve kıyı istemleri `SHAPE_GARDEN_PROMPTS.md` içindedir.

Yeni varlıklar:

- `apps/web/public/island-sea-south-v1.png`: ormanın alt kenarından başlayan kumsal, turkuaz deniz ve adalar.
- `apps/web/public/island-sea-east-beach-v2.png`: sağa devam eden uzun kumsal, üç sahil evi ve kayık iskelesi. Güncel istem: `docs/ISLAND_EAST_BEACH_PROMPT.md`. Eski `island-sea-east-v1.png` yalnızca geçmiş olarak saklanır.
- `work/island-world-reference.png`: kullanıcının içeriğe yönelik referansı; metin ve arayüz görselin parçası yapılmadı.

Yerleşim `apps/web/src/island-world.ts`, ayrı bileşen `apps/web/src/IslandWorldFour.vue`. Orman 3840 × yaklaşık 2161; aşağıdaki deniz bölgesi sağa genişleyerek toplam 5760 × yaklaşık 2161 dünya pikselidir. Dünya yüksekliği yaklaşık 4322 pikseldir. Orman/deniz ve iki deniz görseli arasında 250 kaynak piksel bağlantı şeridi örtüşür; 180–250 arasında CSS maskesi yumuşatır. Kamera üstte 3840, altta 5760 genişliğini izler.

İlk tuval: 1672 × 1191; ormanın en alt 250 pikseli üstte, 941 piksellik yeni alan altta. İkinci tuval: 1086 × 941; ilk deniz bölgesinin sağındaki 250 kaynak piksel solda, 836 piksellik yeni alan sağda.

## Güney deniz bölgesi istemi

```text

Asset type: new fourth-tab forest-to-islands adventure map, southern continuation.
INPUT 1 is the edit target, a 1672 x 1191 canvas. Its top 250 rows are the EXACT bottom edge of the existing forest valley. Only the plain green area below y250 is to be painted; it is 941 pixels tall.
INPUT 2 is the full original forest, STYLE and SCALE reference. Match its soft round cheerful toy-like 3D aesthetic exactly.
INPUT 3 is a USER CONTENT / GEOGRAPHY reference: a beach-and-islands sea world, sandy coast, lagoons, islands with palms, a small lighthouse island, short jetties and a sailboat. Its style is secondary; preserve the forest's style. Do NOT copy its text panel, UI marker, clouds, frame or labels.
Output EXACTLY 1672 x 1191 landscape image. Preserve the illustrated top 250 rows unchanged in size, position, palette and features. Extend only downwards in the blank region. There must be no new sky or horizon.
SEAM AT y250: lime green forest floor continues across the entire seam. The golden forest path entering at x774 must continue naturally to the beach below. The turquoise river entering at x1165 must follow the same direction and banks and empty into a lagoon. Finish the round green tree cut off near x1350 naturally. Do not repeat a cave, bridge, treehouse, target clearing or campsite from the upper map. Do not shift or shrink the input strip.
NEW REGION: A natural forest-to-BEACH-and-ISLANDS sea world. Forest tapers into a broad cream golden sandy coastline across the upper quarter of the NEW region; tree-covered mainland is a modest border at the north, not a bulky harbor district. The forest path winds towards a tiny beach hut and short wooden landing jetty at the coast. The river gradually empties into the turquoise shallows. One or two palms mix with the last broadleaf trees near sandy banks.
Most of the new area (roughly 70%) is inviting vivid cyan turquoise SEA, shallow mint lagoons and distinct ISLANDS. One medium grassy rocky island at middle-left has a small red-and-white striped lighthouse, two very small pastel cottages and a short landing pier, with sandy coves and rounded trees. An elongated SANDY PALM ISLAND in middle-right has spacious pale sand beaches, clusters of softly rounded palms, little grass patches and low smooth rocks, with a tiny short wooden landing. Two small natural sandy rocky palm islets lower down are separated by generous navigable-looking water. A modest blue-and-white sailboat sits between islands; a small wooden rowboat rests near a beach landing. White foam hugs low pale gray lilac rocks, turquoise shallow water gently deepens to blue. Islands have usable open areas and varied sizes and silhouettes. Clear WATER channels separate the mainland and islands, with no massive port infrastructure.
This is island and beach exploration, NOT a busy coastal harbor: no central harbor office, no huge docks, no timber workshop, no rows of quay houses, no industrial boats. Keep buildings sparse and tiny compared to beaches. Avoid three identical circular islands in a row. At the RIGHT edge allow coastal mainland at the upper edge and uninterrupted turquoise sea lower down so the next tile can continue east.
EXACT original forest illustration aesthetic: puffy rounded lime foliage, teal sculpted conifers, bright yellow-lime grass, smooth pale lavender gray rocks, honey orange chunky wood, warm golden paths, smooth palm fronds, saturated turquoise sea with delicate white foam, gentle sunny diffuse light and soft volumetric shading. Same elevated oblique game-map camera and object size. No shift in style, realism, harsh outlines, gritty texture, dense details or flat vector art.
No characters, labels, text, UI badges, numbered circles, selection dot, game markers, clouds, border or watermark. The only existing top strip is preserved; new subject matter is south of it.
```
