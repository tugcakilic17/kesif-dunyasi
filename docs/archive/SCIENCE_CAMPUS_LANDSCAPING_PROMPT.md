## Üretim istemi

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset: seamless illustrated children's game world map tile. Input image is the EDIT TARGET, an approved science academy campus at right/bottom and an unchanged leafy harbor at left. The user LOVES the existing small science trees: preserve their exact design, slim ivory-white trunks and soft faceted rounded BLUE/TURQUOISE crowns, playful gentle crystalline foliage, matched daylight and shadows. They want these trees distributed more widely so the campus no longer feels bare.
Add more of THIS SAME TREE design throughout the SCIENCE LAND ONLY. Make a balanced airy landscape with approximately 25–30 trees total including the existing ones, in loose little groups and occasional pairs, distributed across the whole campus instead of concentrating on the right. Prioritize the empty WESTERN wing and open circular activity court surroundings, the lower/front campus edge, both sides of the school approach, the experiment-stall lawns, solar-zone lawns and path junctions. Use the existing small lawn patches; scatter groupings irregularly with different crown sizes and cyan/blue variations, keeping the same recognizable family. Each small lawn does not need a tree. Add a few small matching geometric blue/turquoise low plants or rounded crystal-like shrubs at the feet of selected groups, very restrained, to soften bare lawns. No ordinary green woodland trees, pine trees, flower beds or neon magic glow. Decoration should visibly span the whole science campus including its left extension and reduce its stark bare appearance, while staying calm and uncluttered for children.
Leave wide passages and the large circular paved activity courts clear for future game activities. Trees stay at normal small landscape scale, roughly the height of those already shown; do not turn this into a dense forest, tree necklace, or enormous crystalline forest. Keep the front door and all science landmarks clearly visible. Preserve the existing large academy, observatory, greenhouse, telescope, solar panels, wind turbine, experiment stall and gears without changes or extra science equipment.
STRICT INVARIANTS: do not move or reshape any land, coastline, widened sea channel, retaining wall, building, bridge, path or court. Keep exact perspective, framing, full aspect ratio, object scale, lighting and soft rounded 3D children's game style. The entire LEFT harbor/forest, its existing ordinary trees, cottages, docks, boats and small islands, plus the Shapes Garden strip at top and its wooden bridge, remain unchanged. Smooth clean science water stays unchanged, no new rocks/shadows or objects in the sea. Only science-campus landscape decoration is edited. No UI, text, labels, border or people.
